## 1. Background

1.1 The Company develops and operates **FiBuKI**, software for pre-accounting for
Austrian one-person businesses (the **"Project"**).

1.2 The Contributor has made and may continue to make contributions to the
Project, including but not limited to the work in the GitHub repository
`yazzbert/FiBuKI-selfhost` and pull request #64 to `felixtosh/FiBuKI`.

1.3 The Company intends to license the Project to the public under the **GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later)**, and to
offer separate commercial licences. To do either lawfully, the Company requires
the rights set out below in all parts of the Project.

1.4 The Parties wish to record those rights, and the rights the Contributor
retains.

## 2. Definitions

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including software source code,
object code, configuration, documentation, tests and related materials, that the
Contributor has submitted or submits to the Project, whether before or after the
Effective Date, and whether submitted by pull request, commit, patch, message or
any other means.

**"Existing Contributions"** means all Contributions made before the Effective
Date, including the approximately 139 commits across approximately 238 files
comprising the Project's self-host stack.

**"Exploitation Rights"** means all rights of use and exploitation in a work
recognised under the Austrian Copyright Act (`Urheberrechtsgesetz`) and equivalent
laws elsewhere, including reproduction, distribution, rental and lending, public
performance, broadcasting, making available to the public, adaptation,
translation, and the creation and exploitation of derivative works, in all media
and formats now known or later developed.

## 3. Grant of Rights

3.1 **Assignment where permitted.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable
law, the Contributor assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and
to the Contributions, including all copyright therein.

3.2 **Exclusive grant of Exploitation Rights.** To the extent that applicable law
does not permit the assignment in §3.1 — which the Parties acknowledge is the
position under Austrian law, where `Urheberrecht` is inalienable — the Contributor
grants the Company an **exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable,
transferable and sublicensable** right to exercise all Exploitation Rights in the
Contributions, unlimited in time, territory and content
(`ausschließliches Werknutzungsrecht`).

3.3 **Right to relicense.** The grants in §3.1 and §3.2 expressly include the
right for the Company to license and sublicense the Contributions **under any
terms of its choosing**, including AGPL-3.0-or-later, any other open source
licence, any source-available licence, and proprietary or commercial licences,
and to change those terms at any time, without further consent from or notice to
the Contributor.

3.4 **Past and future.** This §3 applies equally to the Existing Contributions and
to all future Contributions, with no further instrument required for the latter.

3.5 **Consideration.** The grants above are made in consideration of
`[state the consideration — e.g. the Contributor's shareholding in the Company /
the sum of EUR X / the role described in the separate agreement dated …]`, which
the Contributor acknowledges as adequate.

## 4. Moral Rights

4.1 The Contributor's moral rights (`Urheberpersönlichkeitsrechte`), including the
right to be identified as author and to object to derogatory treatment, are not
transferred and remain with the Contributor.

4.2 To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor agrees not to assert moral
rights against the Company, its licensees or sublicensees in a way that would
prevent the ordinary exploitation of the Contributions under §3, including
modification, combination with other code, and distribution under the licences
contemplated by §3.3.

4.3 The Company will retain the Contributor's authorship attribution in the
Project's version control history and will not misrepresent the origin of the
Contributions.

## 5. Patents

5.1 The Contributor grants the Company a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable, sublicensable licence under any patent claims the
Contributor owns or controls that are necessarily infringed by the Contributions,
alone or in combination with the Project, to make, use, sell, offer for sale,
import and otherwise transfer the Project.

5.2 The Contributor is not obliged to acquire or maintain any patent.

## 6. Rights Retained by the Contributor

6.1 **Licence back.** The Company grants the Contributor a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable and sublicensable licence to use,
reproduce, modify, distribute and otherwise exploit **the Contributor's own
Contributions** for any purpose, including in other projects and for commercial
purposes.

6.2 For the avoidance of doubt, §6.1 covers only the Contributor's own
Contributions. It grants no rights in any other part of the Project, nor in the
Project as a whole, nor in the FiBuKI name or brand.

6.3 The Contributor may continue to state publicly that they authored the
Contributions and may describe that work, subject to §8 (Confidentiality).

## 7. Representations and Warranties

The Contributor represents and warrants that:

7.1 They are the sole author of the Contributions, or otherwise have full right
and authority to make the grants in §3 and §5.

7.2 The Contributions are their original work and, to the best of their
knowledge, do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

7.3 Where any Contribution incorporates third-party material, including open
source dependencies or code, that material is identified in the Project and its
licence terms are compatible with AGPL-3.0-or-later distribution.

7.4 **No employer or third-party claim.** The Contributions were not created
within the scope of any employment, contract, internship, scholarship, or other
relationship under which any third party could claim rights in them; or, where any
such relationship exists, the Contributor has obtained a written waiver or
assignment from that party, a copy of which is attached as **Annex A**.

7.5 They are not subject to any agreement that conflicts with this Agreement.

7.6 Except as stated in this §7, the Contributions are provided **without
warranty of any kind**, and the Contributor is not liable for defects in them.

## 8. Confidentiality

Neither Party will disclose the other's non-public business, technical or
financial information learned in connection with the Project, except where
disclosure is required by law or the information is or becomes public through no
breach of this clause. This clause does not restrict the Contributor's rights
under §6.

## 9. General

9.1 **Governing law.** Austrian law, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules and the
UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** The competent court in `[Vienna / city]`, Austria.

9.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be
replaced by a valid provision that best reflects the Parties' intent, and the rest
of the Agreement remains in force. In particular, if §3.1 is invalid in any
jurisdiction, §3.2 applies in full in that jurisdiction.

9.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement supersedes any prior understanding on its
subject matter. It does not affect any separate shareholding, employment or
services agreement between the Parties.

9.5 **Amendments.** Only in writing, signed by both Parties.

9.6 **Survival.** §3, §4, §5, §6, §7 and §9 survive termination.

9.7 **Counterparts.** May be signed in counterparts, including electronically, each
of which is an original.
